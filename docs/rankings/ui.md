# Online sheets: publishing, boards, profiles and seasons

Current rules, moved out of AGENTS.md so they load only when the task needs them. The decisions behind these screens are in `docs/rankings/strategy.md`. Read before changing `ui/board-*.js`, `ui/account-ui.js`, `ui/profile-ui.js`, `ui/seasons-ui.js`, `ui/player-card.js` or `services/captcha.js`.

- The Saló de la fama's estendard (`seasons-ui.js`: a red cloth with the figure's silhouette, hung from a gold rod) and its season name in gold are an approved decorative exception to the colour roles, used nowhere else.
- Publish prompt («Publica el teu resultat!», `#publish-menu`, publish-ui.js): opens by itself once per eligible run; texts centred. A player with a
  nickname and a live profile here gets no prompt: the same runs (≥ 2 stars) go up by themselves, with nothing on screen (`publish-land.js`
  `publishQuietly`); if that fails (no session, profile gone, network) the prompt opens as for anyone. ⚙ Configuració «Publicació automàtica» (`#settings-quiet-row`, `QUIET_OFF_KEY`), shown only to a player with a nickname here, turns it off: the prompt asks again. On that
  auto opening only, «Ara no» (a decline, ×`MAX_DECLINES` stops it) and quiet «No m’ho tornis a recordar» (`OPTOUT_KEY`, permanent) just
  close it. ⚙ Configuració «Proposta de publicar» (`#settings-prompt-row`) shows only while `promptsOn()` is false, with an «Activa» button that clears both.
- Classificació (`#board-menu`, `ui/board-list.js`; docs/rankings/strategy.md §4): no view tabs (the owner removed «Tots · On soc», 2026-10-21). The title row is a
  button (`#board-figure`: the figure's pixel portrait, name, chevron) that opens «Tria figura» in place of the list (`#board-picker`, the shown figure cream;
  Escape and × come back to the board), then the season line, a flat «Posició 48 de 119» card (gold strip) with one link that reads «Al capdamunt» (an up arrow) while
  the own row is in view, else «A la meva fila» (the arrow towards the row; accessible names «Ves al capdamunt de la classificació» / «Ves a la teva fila»; both labels share the longer one's width, on the place's line from 320 px), and the list opened at the own row (past the first page: a gap row whose «Carrega’n més» fills it).
  With a row the link keeps its place while it has nothing to do (no shift); with no position («12 participants») it collapses until there is somewhere to go.
  An empty open season: the portrait, «Encara no hi ha resultats», what to do, the last season's podium; no history: no «Temporades anteriors» either.
- Cloudflare Turnstile (`src/services/captcha.js`): only when a token is needed (Publica for a new profile, an e-mail code, a Bústia message),
  never ahead of it, so a player who doesn't publish never sees a tick box; never at startup, its iframe shares the main thread on iPhone Safari;
  its tick box only in the centred, non-dismissable `#captcha-menu`, never mid-song; failures never block the game.
  Never render the widget anywhere else. Comparteix never opens by itself: only its results button opens it.
- Sheets whose content loads (Classificacions): fixed height from the first frame; loading/empty/error sit inside the
  list box, never change the sheet's height. An empty open board opened over the bare selector (not over results, Perfil or
  the Saló de la fama) offers «Juga amb el Drac / la Mulassa» (`#board-play`, the sheet's one primary; the hint then says «Publica el primer resultat i estrena la classificació.», not the button's words again): it closes the board
  (not back to Configuració) and leaves that figure selected on the cards, step 1 (`select.show(id)`).
- **Names and profiles** (docs/rankings/strategy.md §6, decisions 10, 33): a name is one active profile's, plain text everywhere: never a «#1234» (no guest tags). A confirmed e-mail earns the tick wherever the name appears (boards, podiums, cards, the share image) and nothing else; a guest has no mark («Convidat» only in its own Perfil block). A guest idle for 30 days can lose its name to somebody else: the profile is then *released* (it keeps its account, results and alias text): its rows on boards, podiums, the hall and cards are faded (`.player-name.released`, the old quieter-guest colour; no extra words in rows), its card adds the note «Aquest nom ara és d’una altra persona.», and when the player returns `my_profile()` says `released`: the Perfil block shows one sentence («Has estat un temps sense jugar i el teu nom ara és d’una altra persona. Tria’n un altre.») with «Tria un altre nom», which opens the profile sheet's name field (`update_alias`, no captcha, no statistics notice); the publish sheet opens empty with the same sentence and sends the chosen name with the publication (a taken one is the usual `taken` error). `submit_result` ignores the alias of an active profile: renaming only goes through `createProfile`. No 7-day temporary profiles and no purge for inactivity. Rows, podium items and the hall carry the opaque `public_id` (`playerOf`, profile-notes.js): the card opens by it (`service.playerCard(publicId)`), never by name. `savedName` (board-list.js) reads the name, the check, `released` and the public id from `<alias key>:identity`, whose public id is the owner key (`ownerKey`); `dropLegacyIdentity` removes the old `leaderboard-tag` key and any cached identity that still carries a tag at start. `refreshIdentity` (`service.profile()`) keeps that cache current at the first idle selector, after a publication and on account events, and clears it when the profile is gone or signed out (no Supabase at page start).
- **Perfil** (`#account-menu`, decision 6; `ui/account-ui.js`): one sheet height from the first frame (like Classificació); while the statistics load, the dancing Mulassa and «Carregant les estadístiques…» (or «No s’han pogut carregar…») sit inside their own room, and the profile block with a name but no answer yet is an invisible guest's block of the same size, so nothing grows when the answers arrive. The profile block (`profile-ui.js`: Crea, Recupera, Confirma el correu, Tria un altre nom for a released profile, Tanca la sessió, Esborra el perfil; each step returns to Perfil), the player's statistics (`stats-view.js`: this device's at once, the server's `my_stats` when it knows more; the days played and streaks are private, only here), the season podiums and the previous dance's record. Statistics live in `game/stats.js` per owner (`none` until a profile exists; what was played without one joins the next profile once, decision 30; a deleted profile takes its numbers, decision 31); `services/stats-sync.js` uploads them (`merge_stats`, a minute apart, after a run, never during a song) only once that profile has seen the statistics notice (`stats-notice-<owner>`: shown on the Crea / first-publication step, or as `#stats-notice-menu` at the first idle selector for a profile made before: the welcome sheet «Nova temporada!» (a ribbon in the estendard's red, then three steps on ribbon medals: 1 Tardor 2026 starts from zero, with a «Saló de la fama» button to the old results; 2 only for a guest, «Conserva el teu nom»: 30 idle days free the name, a confirmed e-mail keeps it, and «Confirma el correu» leads; 3 the statistics in one short paragraph), then «Privadesa» and «D’acord»; any way out (the ×, «D’acord», the hall button) counts as seen, and a quiet publication shows nothing, so it never counts it; the details it no longer says, such as no time of day, no device and deletion with the profile, stay in Privadesa's `data-privacy="leaderboards"`). A name tapped on a board or podium opens the public card (`player-card.js`, decision 18, the owner's «Fitxa breu» 2026-10-21) by its `public_id`: the name (with the released note, if any), the board row it was tapped on (`open(p)` with `p.from`), four facts with drawn icons, the podiums as achievements and «Més estadístiques» unfolding the rest in place (PERFECTE/BÉ/ERROR and falls are public too); never days, streaks, errors per kind or dates. Perfil shows the same blocks in the second person, unfolded, plus the days, streaks and errors per kind.
- **Seasons** (§4-5): one board per figure and season (a quarter named Hivern/Primavera/Estiu/Tardor + year, Europe/Madrid; «Estrena 2026» is the history before them), every level mixed by points; the board's header says the season and its days left. The Saló de la fama (`#seasons-menu`, `seasons-ui.js`) shows one closed season of a figure at a time (the figure through the Classificació sheet's dropdown, `ui/figure-picker.js`), the newest first: its estendard, dates and participants, the podium (the player's row says «TU»), the player's place, points and level in its own band, «Mira la classificació», and «Tria temporada» (the seasons by year, inside the same sheet); desktop puts the podium left and the context right; a dance replaced mid-season gets one quiet line, «Hi ha dos balls · Mira el ball anterior/actual», opening on the dance the player was on; a figure with no closed season shows an empty estendard and the open season. The end-of-season sheet (`#season-end-menu`, decision 17) tells the newest ended season where the profile played: on a podium, that figure on its step and «Has fet podi!» (none: a garland and «Gràcies per fer plaça!»), a row per board with its place, the open season (places are plain numbers, «12 de 48», never gendered ordinals), «Torna a la plaça» and a «Saló de la fama» link (the whole podium lives there, not in this sheet); once per profile and season (`season-seen-<owner>-<season>`); only a season shown is marked seen, older ones follow one at a time. `greeting.js` tells it, then the statistics notice, then a device tip (`device-hint.js`, below), at an idle selector (no sheet, not playing, not in photo mode), at most one of them per visit (page load): the rest wait for the next visit in that order, a new owner's after an account change too (its statistics stay unsent until its notice is seen; offline: later). `figureSeasons` caches `get_seasons` per owner for a minute and drops it on account events. Places 1-3 on every podium and board are one medal, the solid ribbon one (`posDisc`, sheet-kit.js; base.css `.pos.p1-3`, sized by `--pos-size`): never a second medal style.
