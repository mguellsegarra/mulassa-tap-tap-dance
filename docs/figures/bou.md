# The Bou Tradicional in the game

Current rules, moved out of AGENTS.md so they load only when the task needs them. Its music package is `music/bou-tradicional/` (README in Catalan); its research, `research/bestiari/bou-tradicional/`.

- `src/song-bou.json` — **generated** by `node tools/music/build_bou_chart.mjs` from the approved v13 (`music/bou-tradicional/`: timing.json's melody and exact grid; the MP3 is copied byte for byte, never re-encoded). A run counts in like the others' (LEAD, timbal 3 2 1, BALLA!) from 0, its own 8-beat intro (taiko, kit, roll) after BALLA!, as the Mulassa's and the Drac's; `tools/music/build_bou_mobile.py` (revision 19: 18's run-up techno kick removed) fills that intro like theirs (louder hits, shaker, hats, tambourine, a C/G plucked bass; no gaps) (at v13's it was a soft pulse that read as a second count-in) and cuts v13's first intro bar (`cutBeats` 4 in playback.json: the first note at 4 s, not 6; the Mulassa's is at 4.7, the Drac's 5.6), so build_bou_chart.mjs moves timing.json's whole map 2 s earlier. The Bou (UAF's Bou Tradicional, not the Turba's): model `figures/bou/bou.js` (p13, tail pendulum), rig `figures/bou/bou-rig.js`, dance `figures/bou/bou-dance.js` + `figures/bou/bou-feet.js`, applied by `figures/bou/bou-motion.js`, placed on the song clock by `figures/bou/bou-stage.js` and driven each frame by `figures/bou/bou-play.js` (its hooks: `figures/bou/bou-scene.js`). Its colla (`figures/bou/bou-colla.js`, ticked by bou-stage.js `bouCollaTick`): four attendants W/N/S/E 5.5 m out on the plaça's compass (`BOU_COLLA_YAW`: the camera looks in between W and S), no one seated (the Bou's colla is a few people), on the Drac's colla bodies in the colla's correfoc wear (`figures/bou/bou-suit.js` BOU_SUIT: garnet hooded tunics, some hoods up, sunglasses; and a fifth, the capatàs (`BOU_CAPATAS`, near the dance but on none of the four marks), an older short stout man with a black fire hat and the crooked staff with the esquella (a cowbell); research/bestiari/bou-tradicional/dance-integration.md); hidden in free play. Its dance (`figures/bou/bou-dance.js`, the owner's phrase notes 2026-10-24) is keyed per source bar in its colla's compass (the dance frame turns at `BOU_COLLA_YAW`: its W is the W attendant's) with a comic layer on every beat (knee dip, waddle, nod, see-saw; never still); in the final disco (bars 84-91) it spins five and a half turns on the spot, pulsed on the beat, and on the last chord stops dead with its inertia carrying it past and back (`INERTIA`); the disco floor lights again in the final (bars 84-91, its fastest; `bouTechno`, figures/bou/bou-correfoc.js; the run-up starts too slow), with the carretilles and confetti still going (`bouHush`: only the first disco has the plaça to itself); its rushes towards N/S/W/E (`RUSH`) end a hand short of that attendant (tests/figures/bou/bou-colla.mjs: 0.14 m, never touching) in a cartoon skid (`SKIDS`: slide, fishtail, sitting back), and its see-saw heaves one end high (`SAW_RISE`: the low end holds, the high end shows its carrier's legs and trunk); in the festa (bars 72-83, `BOU_CARRY`) the four attendants sprint round it to its flanks (`carryAt`, polar: never through the horns or the tail; they run in through bar 71, grip on its last beat and have it up on bar 72's downbeat, where the music's festa starts; every rush also arrives on a downbeat) and heave it up over their heads at arm's length in one beat, nose up, bucking (`buck` ±0.1 rad) and rocking on every beat (`lift` .75 m, `rear` .3 rad; the carriers' trunks and arms show under the risen hem, bou-rig.js), as in the videos (2xG98OA1QqI 208 s, G_R-ZvzS95k 200 s, Klr8m3U6qzg 216 s), and run it twice round the `LAP` (off round 3 beats after the lift; pulled up with a skid and set down in 2 beats with a bounce, then left still while its colla walks off; its carretilles burn all the way round and never while it is set down, `bouJetsWant`; r 3 m, S -> E -> N -> W, 35 beats, ~2.7 m/s: faster, a carrier's stride outran the other carrier's feet; setting off and pulling up eased: `lapWay`; its carriers' feet then step on every half beat, bou-feet.js `STEP.run`) while the whole plaça cheers (crowd.js `festa`) and up to 12 of those standing round it («que es vegi la serp des del principi») make one file behind it: two side by side at its rump reaching to touch it, the rest on its trail round the ring; they join one at a time from its head after its colla grips it, each the one standing nearest to their place (in from outside: no one crosses the file), and leave one at a time from the tail ~1.7 s before it is lowered, each straight out to the front row (`paradeJoinAt`, `paradeLeaveAt`; tests/figures/bou/bou-festa.mjs); euphoric (a bar arms up waving, a bar clapping, hopping on every beat; crowd.js `conga`, drawn by their stand-ins: representation.js passes it to `animatePerson`'s `upper`, whose walk otherwise took the arms); runners are drawn at their own speed (crowd.js: the 1.6 m/s draw limit left them up to 6 m behind, merging and popping), euphoric (a bar arms up waving, a bar clapping, hopping on every beat; crowd.js `conga`), chasing their place at its own pace (no lag) and dodging it, its colla and each other, a rotllana (`figures/bou/bou-parade.js`, hooked in crowd.js `steer`), then all walk back. The attendants have jointed legs (`stride` looks) and step with planted soles on the half beats (quarter beats sprinting; bou-colla.js `stepColla`); tests/figures/bou/bou-festa.mjs: no sliding sole, no one through the Bou, an attendant, the capatàs or each other. Its festa: rules in `figures/bou/bou-correfoc.js`, drawn by `figures/bou/bou-show.js` (`bouFrame`, after `dracFrame`): the techno part (bars 27-34) turns the plaça to night and its 2 m tiles (`PAVING`) into a 70s multicolour disco floor, every other tile on each beat plus a ring out of the Bou, with nothing else meanwhile (no carretilles, no confetti); a 30 streak lights its carretilles, small tubes on its back (`bou.fire`, figures/bou/bou.js, no rack) that grow in, fire the Drac's jets (`createFire`, its own pool) two pairs and an up tube at a time, and shrink away; its confetti are 💵 banknotes (confetti.js `CONF_LOOK`, `confBills`).
