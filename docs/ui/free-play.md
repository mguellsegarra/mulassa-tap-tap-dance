# Free play (photo mode)

Current rules, moved out of AGENTS.md so they load only when the task needs them. Read before changing `game/photo-control.js`, `game/photo-touch.js`, `ui/photo-*.js`, `ui/free-music.js` or the figures' free-play motion.

- Free play shows one beast at a time, the one driven (no pair, no «Mira la plaça»: it drives from the start, a pending selection the last playable figure); Tab and the desktop bar go round Mulassa → Drac → Bou (`PHOTO_IDS`, photo-control.js), Escape leaves. The Bou driven: `figures/bou/bou-stage.js` `driveBou`, its carriers stepping live (`figures/bou/bou-feet.js` `liveBouFeet`); Space charges head down, Q lifts its head, E stretches the carriers' left legs, R is the half turn; its feet never leave the floor together (no jump, no lift). Looking around parks it where it stopped.
- Photo mode on touch (`COARSE`; laid out and tuned from Genshin/Fortnite/Roblox handcam videos): no figure bar; it always drives (the selection, else the Mulassa). `src/ui/photo-stick.js` draws a full-screen layer: left half = a floating stick (`game/photo-touch.js`: per-pointerId thumbs, radial dead zone + curve, the base follows past the ring, the rim gallops) that walks from the camera while she turns to where she walks (`movePhoto`, `PHOTO.stickX/Y`); right half = drag to look, a swipe across the screen ≈ 190°, queued in `PHOTO.lookDX/Y` and eased in over ~45 ms (touches come at 60 Hz). The jump big where the right thumb rests, Q/E/R on an arc round it with drawn icons per figure (`ui/photo-icons.js`: the Mulassa's cavallito, coça and culada; the Drac's pixel portrait), the swap (Tab) on the right edge as the other figure's portrait. Phones want landscape there: `photoOrient` locks it where Android allows, else the «gira» cover asks (with «Torna al selector»); Safari on an iPhone adds a home-screen tip on every entry. Quick second taps never zoom (`touchend` guard in input.js).
- Photo mode's radio (`src/ui/free-music.js`): one round button paired with the eye (placed from the eye's rect on show/resize; dimmed with it), a list under it: Música del menú, Ball de la Mulassa, Ball del Drac, Ball del Bou, Silenci. M opens/closes it (also in the ? help), 1–5 pick, arrows move, Escape closes it before it drops the figure; opening stops the figure and frees the mouse look, closing gives the keys back to the plaça; a press on the plaça only closes it. «Ara sona» is a dark status pill with the round portrait (no strip, no outline: an exception to the strip rule for notes). Silenci is the speaker's own preference (`setMenuSound`, state.js); picking a track turns it back on. A song plays on its own source (`chip.radio*`, audio.js: never `src`/`t0`/`buf`, so a figure change does not cut it and a run stops it); the menu loop rests under it. An ended song hands over to the next (Mulassa → Drac → Bou → menu loop), the next one loaded 15 s before the end; only the playing song stays decoded. Leaving photo mode fades back to the menu loop; the choice is not stored. On the menus the crowd claps on what sounds (the song's `beats`, else `menuBeat()`); the driven figure never follows it.
