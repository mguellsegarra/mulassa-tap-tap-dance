## Musicians (local review)

The Bou selects the shared `grallers` line-up: Gralla I, Gralla II, gralla baixa and timbal. The instrument and appearance definitions are shared with `tools/dev/bou-musics.html`; inactive formations stay outside the packed crowd's index buffer. Free play keeps the existing no-band convention.

`tools/music/build_bou_percussion.py` replays the frozen arrangement's percussion scheduler and current mobile mix's intro shaping without synthesizing audio. It generates `src/figures/bou/bou-percussion.json`; regenerate it after changing the arrangement. It keeps don/ka/tim/snare/kick/technoKick, combining simultaneous drum layers into two hand strokes; shaker, hats and cymbals do not drive the timbal. The sticks rebound to a raised ready position and strike only on scheduled drum events, with stroke durations in seconds. `bou-percussion.js` evaluates these cues on the song's variable beat grid during play/watch, including the audible intro before the figure starts dancing; the selector keeps following the menu music. The explorer includes the served song to review the same animation closely. Audio files are unchanged. The owner accepted the B6 collar review. Musician-specific checks pass; see the current shared-suite and S10 status below.
