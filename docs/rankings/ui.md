# Online sheets: publishing, boards, profiles and seasons

Current rules, moved out of AGENTS.md so they load only when the task needs them. The decisions behind these screens are in `docs/rankings/strategy.md`. Read before changing `ui/board-*.js`, `ui/account-ui.js`, `ui/profile-ui.js`, `ui/seasons-ui.js`, `ui/player-card.js` or `services/captcha.js`.

- The Saló de la fama's estendard (`seasons-ui.js`: a red cloth with the figure's silhouette, hung from a gold rod) and its season name in gold are an approved decorative exception to the colour roles, used nowhere else.
- Publish prompt («Publica el teu resultat!», `#publish-menu`, publish-ui.js): opens by itself once per eligible run; texts centred. On that
  auto opening only, «Ara no» (a decline, ×`MAX_DECLINES` stops it) and quiet «No m’ho tornis a recordar» (`OPTOUT_KEY`, permanent) both
  open Comparteix next. ⚙ Configuració «Proposta de publicar» (`#settings-prompt-row`) shows only while `promptsOn()` is false, with an «Activa» button that clears both.
- Classificació (`#board-menu`, `ui/board-list.js`; docs/rankings/strategy.md §4): no view tabs (the owner removed «Tots · On soc», 2026-10-21). The title row is a
  button (`#board-figure`: the figure's pixel portrait, name, chevron) that opens «Tria figura» in place of the list (`#board-picker`, the shown figure cream;
  Escape and × come back to the board), then the season line, a flat «Posició 48 de 119» card (gold strip) with one link that reads «Al capdamunt» (an up arrow) while
  the own row is in view, else «A la meva fila» (the arrow towards the row; accessible names «Ves al capdamunt de la classificació» / «Ves a la teva fila»; both labels share the longer one's width, on the place's line from 320 px), and the list opened at the own row (past the first page: a gap row whose «Carrega’n més» fills it).
  With a row the link keeps its place while it has nothing to do (no shift); with no position («12 participants») it collapses until there is somewhere to go.
  An empty open season: the portrait, «Encara no hi ha resultats», what to do, the last season's podium; no history: no «Temporades anteriors» either.
- Cloudflare Turnstile (`src/services/captcha.js`): one silent check for every player after their first run (its results, or the title
  after leaving it; skipped under webdriver): never at startup, its iframe shares the main thread on iPhone Safari;
  its tick box only in the centred, non-dismissable `#captcha-menu`, never mid-song; failures never block the game.
  Never render the widget anywhere else. After publishing, Comparteix opens by itself (`afterPublish`).
- Sheets whose content loads (Classificacions): fixed height from the first frame; loading/empty/error sit inside the
  list box, never change the sheet's height. An empty open board opened over the bare selector (not over results, Perfil or
  the Saló de la fama) offers «Juga amb el Drac / la Mulassa» (`#board-play`, the sheet's one primary; the hint then says «Publica el primer resultat i estrena la classificació.», not the button's words again): it closes the board
  (not back to Configuració) and leaves that figure selected on the cards, step 1 (`select.show(id)`).
- **Names and profiles** (docs/rankings/strategy.md §6, decisions 10, 33): a guest (no confirmed address) shows as «nom#1234» (the tag comes from the server; `appendName`, quieter `#`; «Convidat» on its card); a confirmed e-mail earns the tick wherever the name appears (boards, podiums, cards, the share image) and a clean name chosen once from Perfil («Tria el teu nom»; closing it keeps the `#`). No 7-day temporary profiles: a profile has no deadline. Guests with no sign-in or play for 12 months can be purged (`leaderboard_private.purge_inactive_guests`; verified accounts never); nothing schedules it: `supabase/ops/guest-retention-cron.sql` is a manual dry count, and activating pg_cron needs the owner's explicit approval. `savedName` (board-list.js) reads the name as the boards show it from `<alias key>:identity`; `refreshIdentity` (`service.profile()`) keeps that cache current at the first idle selector, after a publication and on account events, and clears it when the profile is gone or signed out (no Supabase at page start).
- **Perfil** (`#account-menu`, decision 6; `ui/account-ui.js`): one sheet height from the first frame (like Classificació); while the statistics load, the dancing Mulassa and «Carregant les estadístiques…» (or «No s’han pogut carregar…») sit inside their own room, and the profile block with a name but no answer yet is an invisible guest's block of the same size, so nothing grows when the answers arrive. The profile block (`profile-ui.js`: Crea, Recupera, Confirma el correu, Tria el teu nom, Tanca la sessió, Esborra el perfil; each step returns to Perfil), the player's statistics (`stats-view.js`: this device's at once, the server's `my_stats` when it knows more; the days played and streaks are private, only here), the season podiums and the previous dance's record. Statistics live in `game/stats.js` per owner (`none` until a profile exists; what was played without one joins the next profile once, decision 30; a deleted profile takes its numbers, decision 31); `services/stats-sync.js` uploads them (`merge_stats`, a minute apart, after a run, never during a song) only once that profile has seen the statistics notice (`stats-notice-<owner>`: shown on the Crea / first-publication step, or as `#stats-notice-menu` at the first idle selector for a profile made before: the title «Les teves estadístiques» and three short lines with a drawn icon each (counter, person, calendar), then «Privadesa» and «D’acord»; the details it no longer says, such as no time of day, no device and deletion with the profile, stay in Privadesa's `data-privacy="leaderboards"`). A name tapped on a board or podium opens the public card (`player-card.js`, decision 18, the owner's «Fitxa breu» 2026-10-21): the name, the board row it was tapped on (`open(p)` with `p.from`), four facts with drawn icons, the podiums as achievements and «Més estadístiques» unfolding the rest in place (PERFECTE/BÉ/ERROR and falls are public too); never days, streaks, errors per kind or dates. Perfil shows the same blocks in the second person, unfolded, plus the days, streaks and errors per kind.
- **Seasons** (§4-5): one board per figure and season (a quarter named Hivern/Primavera/Estiu/Tardor + year, Europe/Madrid; «Estrena 2026» is the history before them), every level mixed by points; the board's header says the season and its days left. The Saló de la fama (`#seasons-menu`, `seasons-ui.js`) shows one closed season of a figure at a time (the figure through the Classificació sheet's dropdown, `ui/figure-picker.js`), the newest first: its estendard, dates and participants, the podium (the player's row says «TU»), the player's place, points and level in its own band, «Mira la classificació», and «Tria temporada» (the seasons by year, inside the same sheet); desktop puts the podium left and the context right; a dance replaced mid-season gets one quiet line, «Hi ha dos balls · Mira el ball anterior/actual», opening on the dance the player was on; a figure with no closed season shows an empty estendard and the open season. The end-of-season sheet (`#season-end-menu`, decision 17) tells the newest ended season where the profile played: on a podium, that figure on its step and «Has fet podi!» (none: a garland and «Gràcies per fer plaça!»), a row per board with its place, the open season (places are plain numbers, «12 de 48», never gendered ordinals), «Torna a la plaça» and a «Saló de la fama» link (the whole podium lives there, not in this sheet); once per profile and season (`season-seen-<owner>-<season>`); only a season shown is marked seen, older ones follow one at a time. `greeting.js` tells it, then the statistics notice, then a device tip (`device-hint.js`, below), at an idle selector (no sheet, not playing, not in photo mode), at most one of them per visit (page load): the rest wait for the next visit in that order, a new owner's after an account change too (its statistics stay unsent until its notice is seen; offline: later). `figureSeasons` caches `get_seasons` per owner for a minute and drops it on account events. Places 1-3 on every podium and board are one medal, the solid ribbon one (`posDisc`, sheet-kit.js; base.css `.pos.p1-3`, sized by `--pos-size`): never a second medal style.
