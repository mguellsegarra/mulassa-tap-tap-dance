## Known risks, accepted by the owner

- None left from the Bou's ending: its banknote close-up (and the canvas resize behind it, an 84 ms frame on the S10)
  was removed on 2026-11-16 at the owner's request. The Bou ends with confetti, as the Mulassa does.

  owner for 1.4.0; the lever for 1.4.1 is making the letters' canvases at boot, under the cover.
