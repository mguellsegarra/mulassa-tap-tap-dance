# The selector (main menu)

Current rules, moved out of AGENTS.md so they load only when the task needs them. Read before changing `ui/select-*.js`, `game/showreel.js`, `ui/reel-*.js`, the menu framing in `scene/cameras.js` or `styles/select*.css`.

- The desktop selector footer is an explicit exception: pending figures show a native disabled "Pròximament?" button in the reserved difficulty-action slot, with a transparent background and a neutral border and no invitation animation. Touch still hides it.
- Desktop selector navigation uses one 120×44 px brown capsule with a gold outline and cream arrows, centred above the strip, without a counter. It stays hidden until the opening layout has been measured. Its halves stay in place at the ends. With a fine pointer, the cards and navigation keep a fixed vertical position across playable and pending selections, reserving the primary action row; touch keeps swipe navigation and its existing dock movement.
- Town order: `TOWNS` (figures.js) puts Valls first and the rest by Catalan name (`localeCompare` `ca`); the pills, the strip's banners and `FIGURES` all follow it, each town keeping its catalogue order and the playable figures staying ahead of the pending ones; the invitation is last and outside both lists. A figure with `selectorVisible: false` stays in the catalogue (sources, portrait, translations) but has no card; Solsona shows only the Drac and the Gegants Bojos. The pending towns' pills follow the playable ones; the row of pills scrolls and never filters. Every card and pill is shown from the start: the selector has no fold. A pending card sizes by its drawn silhouette's own box (`silhouetteBox`, the viewBox: the grid of the pixel portrait it will get plus the rim) because a pending figure has no pixel portrait yet (it is drawn when the figure becomes playable); a figure that already has one keeps sizing by it. The pending panel shows one centred line under the entity, `locationOf` (figures.js): «Població · Comarca» in Catalunya, «Sineu · Mallorca», «Vinaròs · País Valencià», «Baó · Catalunya Nord», «Fraga · Franja», read from the town's `comarca`, `island` and `territory` fields. Banners keep one size: a name over 10 letters shrinks (`data-long`), «VILAFRANCA» does not.
- Character selection: splash unlock and `toTitle()` enter `G.state = 'select'` directly, over the same plaça and menu loop. Town pills never filter: one scrolls the strip to centre its town's first figure and selects it (no Tots pill); the active pill is always the selected figure's town (it doesn't follow the strip's scroll). Pending real figures retain their name/entity and show inline information in the measured central band, without a dialog: no portrait there (the strip shows it), an invitation whose verb agrees with the name (`aviatText(figure)`) and an open-code note that is the first thing to go on short screens. Only Mulassa and Drac can play; pending selection hides difficulty, play, records and instructions. Figure selection and difficulty are two steps in the same fixed-height dock: "TRIA DIFICULTAT" opens the level choices with the figure's last played level marked (NORMAL the first time; a challenge link's level once), which looks chosen but never shakes until a tap, and "A BALLAR!"; "Canvia de figura" or Escape returns to the cards. Pending figures cannot enter the difficulty step and show no continue button. The action row sits above the card strip, right-aligned on desktop; all cards use identical grid columns, joined image/text panels and a fixed caption height. The host hides both stage models while selection is pending, retaining the last playable song/model state; photo mode shows that last playable figure instead (desktop keeps its figure bar), and leaving it restores the pending selection untouched. The info block carries town/comarca, name, song, the ranking line and "Com es juga" underneath. The song row ends in «Mira el ball» (`#select-watch`, a secondary `.btn` sized to the row), the figure's whole dance played by itself (ui/watch-ui.js, `startWatch`; a drag, the wheel or a pinch turns and zooms its play camera, the dance and music never stop, and ORBIT.idle s after letting go it eases back to the camera's own shot: `look`/`lookStep`, orbit.js); it left Com es juga, where nobody found it. Phones put it beside the song chip, outside it, at the chip's height (38 px, a 44 px slop; the chip is the inner `.sel-song-plate`), the play alone under 375 px; a narrow desktop column (≤ 470 px: 900-1100 px windows, an iPad) keeps it beside the song as the play alone (44 px), with a 30 px disc, 6 px gaps and a 14 px credit so the credit keeps one line (under the song looked wrong). The ranking line (`#select-rank-tile`, `rankText` in `ui/select-standings.js`; it replaced the corner podium) is one tappable 44 px row under the song with the board icon and a chevron: «Posició 18 de 46 · Tardor 2026» when the profile has a place on the selected figure's open board (no gendered ordinal), else «Classificació · 46 participants», «Classificació · encara buida», or «Classificació» before the answer (a quiet pulsing bar of the line's width until then, `data-loading`); it opens Classificació on the selected figure (`onBoard(figure)`); hidden when no board is configured, and with the selector in photo mode. Desktops show it as the standings card's header (same words, no duplicate), darker than its lighter body; the card hugs its content: until the board answers, a shimmer skeleton (`select-standings.js`, opacity/transform only, flat under reduced motion) takes the shape of the last answer seen for that figure and owner (memory only; the first time, the owner's last answer shown for another figure, else the fullest shape: no grow for the placeholder then shrink for the answer), so the answer rarely moves it; another board (a figure or owner change) or the first answer after the placeholder fades the whole card out (120 ms), swaps it while unseen, new size included, and fades it in (180 ms; a height ease read as jumps): the old board stays during the fade out and the newest answer is the one that comes in (no answer yet: it waits unseen up to 350 ms, so a prompt one needs one fade, a slow one fades the placeholder in, then itself); at once under reduced motion or while the info column is still invisible (back from a pending figure); the same board's answer swaps the skeleton for the rows in place while they cross-fade, never an empty band under the last row or foot; when the column is short the rows go first, then step 2's level row, never the header. Step 2 has no rows: the header and the foot's two gaps («A 59 punts del número 17», «A 78.749 punts del primer»), the level row in the rows' room. Desktop info column: it ends 16 px before the strip's first banner at its rest place (`--info-w`, `measure()`; never under a 300 px panel, so 900 px windows keep 300), the name and song rows scale with it (`cqi`), the standings drop the % column in a narrow column. The three medals carry a flat «Els teus rècords» label; the medals never shrink to one line: board rows, then the foot, give way first, the medals only when even the header alone would not fit; a level with no record shows «Sense rècord» on the points' line and keeps the % line's empty slot, so the first record shifts nothing. Short portrait cards (≤ 100 px tall, 320×568) put the portrait beside the caption instead of hiding it. In step 2 only, a row shaped like the song's (`ui/level-stats.js`, under the song) gives the chosen level's record on this device, labelled («El teu rècord a NORMAL: 72.310 punts», its stars and %; the points alone did not say what they were); no record: «Encara no tens rècord a DIFÍCIL» over «El primer ball que acabis serà el teu rècord» (whose record, and why none); in a 308–324 px column the subtitle goes, then the title shortens to «Sense rècord a DIFÍCIL», before the row would. No board data in it (the owner removed «a N punts del número X»). Each line stays one line and the info block never scrolls for it: desktops' 18 px panel rhythm tightens to 12 first, then the points go, then the song's credit (its disc 32 px), then the board card's foot goals, then the song row above it (`data-snug`: 900×700, 1280×600, 900×600), then the whole row; the ranking header and the level row always stay. Under 641 px of height the desktop name scales by 9.4cqi (from 26 px), so «Mulassa de Valls» keeps one line in 900×600's 300 px column. On desktops step 2's info column runs down to the level card (`--info-dock`), not to the dock's top. The same 12 px rhythm applies in either step whenever the info block would otherwise scroll (a two-line name in the 360 px column at 900×700). Desktops: the info column ends 12 px before the strip's first banner and is never narrower than 308 px; where a centred strip would put that banner further left (under ~1180 px wide) the strip moves right instead (`--strip-l`, measure()). With a fine pointer the strip's box itself is constant (it starts at the page gutter, `--strip-x`), and its left part under the column (the column's edge + 12, `--strip-clip`) is clipped, folded or unfolded: a pending figure or the fold's card re-lays the layout without moving the box (no layout shift; `select-flip.js` slides the cards and buttons), unfolded cards scroll inside it, never visible under the column, every name of the standings card stays clickable (the column sits above the dock), and the column keeps its height either way (≥ 2000×900 it runs down to the foot), and 900–919 px windows ≥ 661 px tall use 200 px cards so both fit. Short portrait phones (≤ 519 × 501–620 px) give the level card the landscape phones' compact size so it fits the clamped dock. The bottom strip has town banners, figure cards, remembered per-figure difficulty and "A BALLAR!". Each town has one accent (`--town`, select-cards.css): its banner's cloth (Valls keeps its white/red/white flag, the invitation the senyera) and, at one fixed OKLCH lightness, its cards' and photo avatars' tint. It is an editorial accent of the game's own palette, not an official municipal colour and not a colla's shirt (some were chosen near a colla's, listed in docs/figures/figure-catalog.md; the arms are on the pills). First runs enter practice through `choose`; `__dbg.choose` keeps direct entry. The inline pending card opens the existing feedback flow with `aviatDraft(town)` or offers mail. Arrows rove visible cards; there is no back-to-title loop. `setupSelect` exposes `figure`, calls `onSelect(figure)` only for playable figures when opened or changed, and `onPlay(level, figure)`; sheets-setup wires the callback to `setSelectedFigure` and passes figure plus level on play. Flow reads the active song adapter, stores records per figure and stamps run/share identity. Both figures have their own board and rank tiles (`standing(figure.id)`, one board per figure and season); the share image asks for the played figure's.
- **Selector showreel** (`game/showreel.js`; history, rejected versions and validation traps: `.agents/skills/mulassacat-camera/`): on the selector a playable figure dances its real song dance (not the old beat demo) behind the cards, looping, on the menu loop's beat (the menu music stays: the Mulassa runs at 93-100% of her speed, the Drac ~1.3x), filmed as a dance film, not a pop video (the first version, a cut every 2.7 s with swinging, pushing shots, hurt the eyes; Astaire's long, open takes): `SHOWREELS` lists per figure [song beat, shot, 'cut' | 'glide'] (`SHOT_KINDS`: angle round `CAM_AZ` within ±0.8 rad, height 0.22-0.42 rad (low: the crowd and façades behind her, not a field of tiles on a Studio Display), share of the band she fills, 0.6-0.8: her whole body with air; nearly held), a shot per part of the dance (16-36 beats, 6-14 s), a cut only at the big changes (≤ 5 a loop), never hard: a dissolve (`ui/reel-fade.js`, `#reel-fade` between the two canvases: the outgoing shot rendered once more in its own camera, copied, and faded out over the new one in `REEL_FADE_MS` 0.5 s (0.9 felt slow), live: each frame of the fade renders the dance again from the outgoing camera (`reelFadeLive`; a frozen frame over her turning read as two of her), except a figure change, a plain cut (`REEL.fresh`: the outgoing frame would already show the new figure in the old shot, two shot changes; Studio Display), and every cut sits where the figure stands still (the Mulassa's a bar off its part's start: 84, 140, 204); the hard cuts read as abrupt), a `REEL_GLIDE` 18-beat (~7 s) eased glide of at most `REEL_GLIDE_AZ` 0.6 rad to the next angle at the others (big swings made him dizzy on a Studio Display; the view turns ≤ ~0.15 rad/s, a seventh of the screen a second), every change on a bar, never the same shot twice in a row; a fresh selection starts on her first move. The Drac's reel is cut for comedy: a fresh selection opens on the wide shot the moment it sets off (a close-up first read as a framing bug); each loop after it opens on a short close-up (`SHOT_KINDS.close`, 8 beats, one of its heads, `part: 'head'`) while it waits, the charge at the lens low from the front held until it stops dead, then its stare in close-up, the dizzy spins from above uncut; close-ups are a long lens from the orbit's nearest (`zoomMenu`'s `ZOOM_TELE`, cameras.js `partOutline`), cut in and out, only where its root stands still (tests/figures/common/showreel.mjs samples drac-path through drac-time). `frameMenuCamera(…, reel)` takes the shot: angle and size at once on a cut, final in the cut's own frame (the figure's matrices updated, measured, re-aimed at her outline's middle, measured again; the height limit at the new distance: any correction a frame later reads as a second cut), follow and lens springs `MENU_T.reel` 0.7 s inside a shot, still framed in `sheets.select.band`; no drag, wheel or pinch moves it on the selector (`homeLocked`, input.js; free play keeps them); when the reel stops (photo mode, a pending card) the orbit gets back the height and distance it had before. The dance clock (`followBeat`) never freezes and never skips: with the loop silent or standing still (a suspended context) it carries on at the loop's tempo, and back on the music (the audio clock settles ~0.4 s off after a tab switch) it catches up by running at most `REEL_CATCH` 15% faster or slower, no jump, no cut. Reduced motion: one still shot. Off in photo mode, on pending cards and with `?still`; tests/figures/common/showreel.mjs. Idle screen (`ui/reel-idle.js`, Netflix's trick): untouched for `REEL_IDLE.after` 10 s (no sheet, not photo mode), `body.reel-idle` fades the selector and its corner row out in 0.4 s and `body.reel-wake` back in 0.25 s (eased, select-sizes.css; 1.2 s drew the eye) and the camera frames her in the room the UI left (`idleBand`, a `soft` band; always framed where no UI covers her): the whole screen inside the safe areas, a portrait screen low in the dock's room; cameras.js eases into it in ~2 s (`MENU_T.soft`) and back out quickly (`MENU_T.wake`), as the UI returns in 0.25 s; any touch, move, wheel or key brings it back, and so does leaving the page (another tab or app, the window's blur: everything pauses, and coming back the selector is up and the time away never counts), and the press (or key) that wakes it only wakes it: it, its up and its click never reach the game. On the selector anyone of the crowd or the collas within `LENS_CLEAR.near` 3.4 m of a low lens (under 7.5 m) shrinks away (`lensShrink`, framing.js; crowd.js, drac-colla.js), as the backdrop's figures do; and the showreel's azimuth keeps `HELPER_CLEAR` 0.5 rad off any colla helper between the lens and her (`clearOfColla`, cameras.js; never over an attendant's shoulder), easing at most `HELPER_CLEAR.speed` 0.12 rad/s.
- **Selector camera** (`frameMenuCamera` / `frameMenuLens`, cameras.js; `menuFit`, `envelope` in framing.js): the selected figure fills 86% of the free rect `sheets.select.band` measures (`top`/`bottom` under the info block and above the dock, `hardTop` = the pills' floor, `left`/`right`: right of a side info panel), read from her live outline (14 support points per mesh through each mesh's matrix: no DOM reads per frame, no feedback from the lens), by dolly and a two-axis lens shift (view offset, never a tilt); where the square stops the dolly short (a short desktop band wants ~23 m, the façades allow 15-17) the lens widens the rest of the way, eased, up to `ZOOM_MAX` (`zoomMenu`; other cameras restore the base lens). A short band (320×568) keeps her readable: at least 55% of the room under the pills, feet on the dock's edge, rising behind the info's lower rows. Her extents go through an envelope (up 0.35 s, down 4 s), her middle and feet through a 1.2 s average, the shift through a 0.9 s spring, the focus follows her outline's middle (1 s spring), and the idle azimuth no longer turns with her heading (both dances spin). On the selector itself no drag, wheel or pinch steers it (the showreel's camera; `homeLocked`, input.js); free play has its own chase camera (`framePhotoCamera`), and the menu camera takes over again when it ends. Coming back from another camera (results) it adopts that pose and eases in.
